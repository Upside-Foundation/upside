# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security issues privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of
this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Please do not open a
public issue, pull request, or discussion for a security problem.

A useful report includes the affected version or commit, the steps to
reproduce the issue, and its expected impact. A proof of concept helps us
confirm the problem faster.

## What to expect

1. Maintainers acknowledge the report through the private reporting channel.
2. Maintainers investigate the report and coordinate further information with the reporter.
3. We develop and test a fix. Some issues take longer than others.
4. Consensus changes require coordinated activation through governance and validator upgrades. We publish the fix and credit the
   reporter in the release notes, unless the reporter prefers to stay
   anonymous.

## Disclosure

Please keep the details of a vulnerability private until a fix is released
and deployed, give us reasonable time to address it, do not exploit it beyond
what is needed to demonstrate it, and do not disrupt the network or its
users.
